Guodong Liu (Eric Liu) 🚀

AI & CS & EE  ·  Nanjing, China

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in computer science at the State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology of Nanjing University. I hope to do some meaningful things that build a learning and research path from sensing, recognition, and cognition to embodied intelligence and AI agents.

My current research interests include

  1. Embodied Intelligence (RL, XLA/VLA/WM, Robot), and Physical AI;
  2. Edge/Sensing LM (Low-Bit LM, Model Compression), and Agents;
  3. AI for Medicine;
  4. Machine Learning, Representation Learning, Generative Model (Diffusion / Flow Matching / AR);
  5. Multi-modal Intelligence (Vision, Speech/Ultrasound/Acoustics, mmWave, Accelerometer, WiFi, and so on), and HCI.
